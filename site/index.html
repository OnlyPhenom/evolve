Module 01 · Investify

The Pit Wall

Duration
~60 min
Rounds
5
Budget
Rs 100,000
Team
4 players

Overview

Your team sits on the pit wall of a struggling racing company with Rs 100,000 of play money. Buy assets that could be worth more later, protect them, strike deals with rival teams, then ride out the surprises. The team whose money grows the most wins.

Format

  • Buy: a Safe Pick, a Risky Pick and a Fixer-Upper, and lock away emergency cash.
  • Protect: buy crash insurance or a secret report on the surprise cards.
  • Trade: sell, swap or team up with other teams, up to three deals.
  • Surprises: three cards such as Market Boom or Supply Chain Collapse change what everything is worth.
  • Count up: final money against Rs 100,000 gives your growth score.

Team roles

Lead Investor, Treasurer, Risk Officer and Dealmaker.

Judging

Highest growth on Rs 100,000 wins, and the Board announces the top three. Cash you keep earns a 5% bonus, so saving is a strategy, not a waste.

Module 02 · Brand and Media

The Media Paddock

Duration
~60 min
Team
4 players
Games
2
Points
200

Overview

The business side of Formula 1. Build your racing company's brand, win over a sponsor and protect your reputation when the spotlight turns hostile. Build the brand. Win the sponsor. Survive the spotlight.

Game 1: The Sponsor Lap

  • Draw a target audience, an advertising challenge and a brand constraint.
  • Create an edited video ad for your racing company that features the EVOLVE 4.0 sponsor.
  • Screen it (up to 2 min), explain your campaign, then give a 1-minute sponsor pitch.

Game 2: The Press Conference

  • Receive a surprise crisis and get 5 minutes to prepare.
  • Deliver a 30-second official statement.
  • Take 2–3 minutes of questions from judges playing journalists and stakeholders, then close with your action plan.

Judging

Each game is scored out of 100 on audience understanding, creativity, sponsor value, clarity under pressure and teamwork. Ties go to the higher Press Conference score. The sponsor also secretly picks one team as its partner, revealed with a bonus at the closing ceremony.

Module 03 · Leadership and Development

The Leadership Grid

Duration
45–55 min
Roles
4
Format
Simulation
Points
20

Overview

Twenty minutes before the race, a critical engine part fails its safety check and the approved replacement is late. Your team is the leadership group that has to decide what happens next, without ever compromising safety.

Format

  • Briefing: learn the scenario and take your roles.
  • First call: discuss and submit one decision.
  • Race Control update: bad weather brings the start forward. Adapt.
  • Final call: your decision, three actions and who owns each.
  • Debrief: explain your choices and hear the judges' feedback.

Team roles

Team Principal, Finance Director, Operations Director and Communications Director.

Judging

Scored out of 20: decision-making, teamwork, adaptability, and accountability and execution, each from 0 to 5.

Module 04 · Team Building

The Paddock Exchange

Duration
35–45 min
Tiers
4
Lots
25 items
Max wins
4 a team

Overview

A live auction of 25 F1-themed items: helmets, model cars, trophies and more. You bid from your team's wallet, which carries across the whole championship, and every item hides a point value revealed only after the hammer falls.

Format

  • Items go up one by one, with 30 seconds to act on each lot.
  • Once your team wins four items, you stop bidding.
  • A different teammate holds the paddle after every win.
  • Hidden tiers: Common 10–25, Rare 30–55, Epic 60–85, Legendary 90–120 points.

Judging

Most points wins. 1st earns 25 championship points, 2nd 21 and 3rd 18, down to 1 point for 16th–20th. On a tie, the team with more budget left ranks higher.

Module 05 · Karting

The Grand Prix

Duration
2–2.5 hrs
Racers
1 a team
Format
Heats + final
Prizes
Top 3

Overview

The Formula 1 theme leaves the room and hits the track. Each team sends one representative to a live karting challenge, racing through timed qualifying heats and a final for the highest finishing position.

Format

  • Nomination: each team names one driver before the event. Drivers must meet the track's eligibility and safety requirements.
  • Briefing: a mandatory safety and race briefing covers the track, rules, penalties and the race format.
  • Qualifying: drivers race in groups in timed sessions, and the fastest lap times go through to the final.
  • The final: the fastest qualifiers race for the podium.
  • Podium: the top three are announced and recognised on a podium.

Judging

Official finishing positions, verified by the track's race officials, decide 1st, 2nd and 3rd. The top three drivers earn prize money that goes straight into their team's virtual wallet for the rest of the championship.
